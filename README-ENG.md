# How This Project Started

For many years, I constantly designed ideas in my mind.

However, I never turned them into reality.

Because everything depends on the opportunities and resources available.

Every person must know their own limits.

When people understand their own limits, nobody else has to remind them.

After experiencing the pandemic, wars, and finally the earthquake, I realized something that completely changed my perspective.

Just like everyone else...

One day I will die.

Sooner or later.

That is an unavoidable fact.

Then I asked myself a simple question.

**What will I leave behind for humanity after I am gone?**

What have I actually built that could benefit other people?

Yes...

I had many project ideas.

But what should I build?

We live in the age of information.

We live in the age of technology.

What could I create that would truly provide the greatest benefit to humanity?

An application?

A game?

A website?

None of those.

None of them reflected my beliefs, my mindset, or my vision.

That is why I decided to build Artificial Intelligence.

I spent a long time studying and working toward that goal.

However, since I was not experienced enough in AI development, I encountered two major obstacles.

1. Extremely powerful hardware requirements.
2. The enormous financial cost required to maintain such infrastructure.

I designed the architecture.

Then I stopped.

Not because I gave up...

But because I didn't want to spend years on something I simply couldn't afford at the time.

# The Local Editor Idea

Later, I decided to develop a completely local code editor.

I started working on it.

Over time, many well-known open-source projects appeared.

Since I was working entirely alone, progress was naturally slow.

Meanwhile, the earthquake aftershocks continued.

Power outages became a normal part of everyday life.

Eventually, my storage drive failed.

Everything I had built was lost.

I did not give up.

I started over.

This time I used AI-assisted development.

Unfortunately, due to an AI-generated mistake—and because I had no backup—I lost everything once again.

Today, I have started over for the third time.

However, this time not as an editor.

Instead...

As an **Agent Operating System (Agent OS).**

Technology keeps evolving.

People keep evolving.

Building only another editor no longer made sense to me.

That is why I chose to build an Agent Operating System.

I especially want to emphasize that this project should not be confused with products such as "Agent Code."

This project will run entirely locally.

It will be completely closed source.

It will also be released as a paid product.

I want to make this point very clear.

The reasons that forced me to abandon my previous AI project still exist today.

My goal is **not** to build an Agent OS simply to sell it and make money.

My goal is much larger than that.

This project is only one step toward a far bigger vision that will require years of work, significant resources, and a budget that I cannot possibly provide alone.

For that reason, this project will remain closed source and commercial.

# Current Project Status

At this point, the project is approximately **35–40% complete**.

Everything achieved so far has been developed using only free AI APIs.

As many people already know, free AI models are primarily released to help train and improve future generations of models.

Unfortunately, this makes them far from ideal for building a project of this scale.

As a result, my development process becomes significantly slower.

However, performance is not my only concern.

Security and privacy are equally important.

Although I describe this as a closed-source project, the reality is different.

Because I currently depend on external AI services, parts of my source code inevitably leave my local environment.

For a project that is intended to remain completely private, this is a serious problem.

My goal is simple.

I want to complete this project entirely with local AI models.

To make that possible, I need a workstation capable of running state-of-the-art large language models locally.

The hardware I am aiming for includes:

- Three NVIDIA RTX 6000 GPUs
- 1 TB of DDR5 RAM
- A motherboard capable of supporting this configuration
- A high-end processor
- High-speed SSD storage

Many people may wonder why I need such an extreme system.

The answer is straightforward.

Today, this project already contains approximately **150,000 lines of source code**.

By the time development is finished, I estimate the codebase will reach somewhere between **400,000 and 500,000 lines of code**, although the final size may be larger or smaller.

Managing a project of this scale with small language models is simply not practical.

Technically, I could continue using smaller models and spend years moving forward little by little.

Eventually, I would probably finish it.

The real question is different.

Will I still be alive by then?

And even if I am...

Where will technology be by that time?

How far will large technology companies have progressed?

Time is my biggest limitation.
